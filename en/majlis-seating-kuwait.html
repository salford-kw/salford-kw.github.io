What we make

A majlis is the traditional Arabic sitting room, with seating that runs along the walls. We make the seating for it in our own workshop: foam back cushions, back cushions with a decorative wooden frame, ready floor seating, and full diwaniya seating in modern or formal styles.

Frequently asked questions

Do you make majlis seating to order?

Yes. We specialise in custom decor majlis, back cushions and Kuwaiti diwaniya seating, made to the size and style you choose.

What fabrics are available?

A wide range including jacquard, chenille and velvet, plus stain-resistant fabrics for everyday use, in many colours.

Do you measure at home?

Yes. Measuring and inspection at home are free in every area of Kuwait, so the majlis is made to the correct size.

How long does a majlis take to make?

It depends on the size and design: back cushions take about 3 days and a wooden decor majlis about 15 days.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, we give a warranty on the quality of the tailoring and stitching. Call +965 5594 3343 for the details.
