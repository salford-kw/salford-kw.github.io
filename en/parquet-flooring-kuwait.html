Why synthetic parquet

The material is made to resist moisture and to take daily use without cracking or swelling, unlike natural wood parquet, which is easily affected by moisture. It suits bedrooms, living rooms and offices.

Shades and price

We carry a wide range of wood shades, light and dark, and show them to you during the visit so you can choose what suits your interior. Synthetic parquet starts from 9 KD per metre. Measuring at home is free, and the final price depends on the area and the shade you choose. Pay by KNET, Wamd or cash.
