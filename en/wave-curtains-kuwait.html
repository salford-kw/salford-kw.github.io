What a wave curtain is

The fabric is sewn onto a special wave tape that makes it fall in regular, equal folds along the track. The folds cover the track, so the curtain looks neat and tailored compared with traditional pleats. It is the style most often chosen for living rooms and majlis rooms.

Wave, roller or blackout?

Wave curtains are folded fabric with an elegant look for living rooms and majlis. Roller blinds are a single panel that rolls up with a simple movement and suit practical spaces. Blackout is a dense, opaque fabric that blocks the light and suits bedrooms. A wave curtain can also be made in blackout fabric, which combines the tailored look with full darkness, and it can be paired with a sheer layer.
